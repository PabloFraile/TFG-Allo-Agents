```python
            y_real[idx1_10] = a_real_10 + t_real_10
            y_imag[idx1_10] = a_imag_10 + t_imag_10
            y_real[idx2_10] = a_real_10 - t_real_10
            y_imag[idx2_10] = a_imag_10 - t_imag_10
```
